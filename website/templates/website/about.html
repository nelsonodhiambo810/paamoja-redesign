What Drives Us

Our Mission

To empower young people in Mathare Valley with knowledge, skills, and confidence — enabling them to become agents of change in their families and communities.

Our Vision

A Mathare Valley where every young person regardless of background has access to quality life skills education, dignified livelihoods, and a platform to lead.

Our Values
  • Community First
  • Radical Inclusion
  • Integrity in Service
  • Youth-Led Change
  • Dignity & Respect
